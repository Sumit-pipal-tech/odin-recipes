Aloo Paratha

Description

Aloo Paratha is a popular North Indian flatbread stuffed with a flavorful mixture of mashed potatoes and spices. It is commonly enjoyed for breakfast or lunch.

Served with yogurt, butter, or pickle, Aloo Paratha is a delicious and satisfying meal loved across India.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 3 boiled potatoes, mashed
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 2 tablespoons coriander leaves, chopped
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • Ghee or oil for cooking

Steps

  1. Mix the wheat flour with water and knead into a soft dough.
  2. In a bowl, combine mashed potatoes, green chili, spices, coriander, and salt.
  3. Divide the dough and potato mixture into equal portions.
  4. Roll a dough ball, place the potato filling inside, and seal it.
  5. Gently roll it into a flat paratha.
  6. Cook on a hot tawa until both sides are golden brown.
  7. Apply ghee or oil while cooking.
  8. Serve hot with yogurt, butter, or pickle.
