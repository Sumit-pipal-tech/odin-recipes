Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken Biryani is a famous South Asian rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, tender chicken, and a blend of aromatic spices. It is known for its rich flavor and delicious aroma.

This dish is often served on special occasions and is enjoyed with raita, salad, or a boiled egg on the side.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 2 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a large pot and fry the onions until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for one minute.
  4. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.
  5. Add chicken, yogurt, spices, and salt. Cook until the chicken is tender.
  6. Boil the rice until it is about 70% cooked and drain it.
  7. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture.
  8. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 20–25 minutes.
  9. Garnish with coriander and mint leaves.
  10. Serve hot with raita or salad.
