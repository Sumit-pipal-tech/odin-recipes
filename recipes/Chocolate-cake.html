Chocolate Cake

Description

Chocolate Cake is a rich and delicious dessert loved by people of all ages. It is made with cocoa powder, flour, sugar, and other simple ingredients to create a soft and moist cake.

Perfect for birthdays, celebrations, or simply satisfying a sweet craving, this classic chocolate cake is easy to make and always a crowd-pleaser.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup warm water

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Grease and flour a cake pan.
  3. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and baking soda in a bowl.
  4. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla extract. Mix until smooth.
  5. Gradually stir in the warm water.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan.
  7. Bake for 30–35 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  8. Allow the cake to cool before serving.
