Masala Chai

Description

Masala Chai is a traditional Indian tea made by brewing black tea with milk, sugar, and a blend of aromatic spices. It is one of the most popular beverages in India and is enjoyed throughout the day.

The combination of tea and spices creates a warm, flavorful drink that pairs perfectly with snacks such as biscuits, pakoras, or sandwiches.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons black tea leaves
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • 2 green cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 small piece of ginger, grated
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 2 cloves

Steps

  1. Bring the water to a boil in a saucepan.
  2. Add the ginger, cardamom, cinnamon, and cloves.
  3. Let the spices simmer for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add the tea leaves and boil for another minute.
  5. Pour in the milk and add the sugar.
  6. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil.
  7. Simmer for 2–3 minutes until the tea becomes flavorful.
  8. Strain the tea into cups.
  9. Serve hot and enjoy.
