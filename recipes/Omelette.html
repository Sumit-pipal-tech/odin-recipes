Omelette

Description

An Omelette is a simple and nutritious dish made from beaten eggs cooked in a frying pan. It is a popular breakfast choice around the world because it is quick and easy to prepare.

You can customize your omelette with vegetables, cheese, herbs, or spices to suit your taste. It is delicious when served with toast or a fresh salad.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon milk (optional)
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon coriander leaves, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon oil or butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat them well.
  2. Add milk, onion, green chili, coriander, salt, and pepper.
  3. Mix all the ingredients together.
  4. Heat oil or butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
  6. Cook for 2–3 minutes until the bottom is set.
  7. Fold the omelette in half and cook for another minute.
  8. Transfer to a plate and serve hot.
