Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are soft, fluffy flat cakes made from a simple batter of flour, milk, eggs, and baking powder. They are a popular breakfast food enjoyed around the world.

Pancakes can be served with syrup, honey, butter, fruits, or chocolate sauce, making them a versatile and delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk together the egg, milk, butter, and vanilla extract.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients and mix until smooth.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour a small amount of batter onto the pan.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip the pancake.
  7. Cook the other side until golden brown.
  8. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
