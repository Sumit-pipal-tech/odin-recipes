Paneer Butter Masala

Description

Paneer Butter Masala is a rich and creamy Indian curry made with paneer cubes cooked in a flavorful tomato and butter-based gravy.

It is one of the most popular North Indian dishes and is often served with naan, roti, or rice.

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cubed
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/4 cup fresh cream
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Heat butter in a pan and sauté the onions until golden.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for one minute.
  3. Add tomato puree and cook until the oil separates.
  4. Mix in red chili powder, garam masala, and salt.
  5. Add cream and stir well.
  6. Add paneer cubes and cook for 5 minutes.
  7. Serve hot with naan, roti, or rice.
