Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish consisting of a baked flatbread topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and various toppings.

This simple homemade pizza recipe is perfect for beginners and can be customized with your favorite ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni slices
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Oregano

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a baking tray.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Add mozzarella cheese and pepperoni slices.
  5. Sprinkle oregano on top.
  6. Bake for 12–15 minutes until golden brown.
  7. Remove from the oven, slice, and serve hot.
