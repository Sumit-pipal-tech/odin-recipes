Poha

Description

Poha is a popular Indian breakfast dish made from flattened rice, spices, onions, and peanuts. It is light, nutritious, and easy to prepare.

This delicious dish is especially popular in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh and is often served with lemon and fresh coriander.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups poha (flattened rice)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1/4 cup peanuts
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • 1 lemon

Steps

  1. Rinse the poha gently and drain the water.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add mustard seeds.
  3. Add peanuts and cook until lightly roasted.
  4. Add onions and green chilies and sauté until soft.
  5. Add turmeric powder and salt.
  6. Add the poha and mix everything well.
  7. Cook for 2–3 minutes on low heat.
  8. Garnish with coriander leaves and lemon juice.
  9. Serve hot.
