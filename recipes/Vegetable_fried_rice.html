Vegetable Fried Rice

Description

Vegetable Fried Rice is a quick and flavorful dish made with cooked rice, mixed vegetables, and simple seasonings. It is a popular meal that can be enjoyed on its own or served with other dishes.

This recipe is easy to prepare and is a great way to use leftover rice. The colorful vegetables add both nutrition and taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1/2 cup carrots, chopped
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1/2 cup capsicum, chopped
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large pan or wok.
  2. Add garlic and onion and sauté for 1–2 minutes.
  3. Add carrots, peas, and capsicum and cook until slightly tender.
  4. Add the cooked rice and mix well.
  5. Pour in the soy sauce and stir thoroughly.
  6. Add salt and black pepper to taste.
  7. Cook for another 2–3 minutes while stirring.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy.
